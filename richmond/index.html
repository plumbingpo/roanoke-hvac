Two common situations call for quick action from you before our technician gets there. The first is an iced-over air conditioner: turn the system off at the thermostat and set the fan to "on" so the moving air thaws the coil, because continuing to run it can overflow the drain pan and hurt the compressor. The second is a furnace that short-cycles or gives off a burning smell. Turn it off at the service switch and call us. That small step can save you from a much larger repair.

Getting More Years From Your HVAC System in Richmond

The best way to avoid a mid-season breakdown is routine maintenance. We recommend that homeowners replace filters every one to three months, keep vegetation trimmed two feet back from the outdoor condenser, rinse the condenser coil each spring, and schedule a professional tune-up twice a year, cooling in spring and heating in fall, so small problems get caught while they're still inexpensive.

The age of your equipment matters just as much. With regular service, a central air conditioner usually runs 12 to 15 years, a furnace 15 to 20, and a heat pump 10 to 15. Once a system passes that range and starts showing warning signs, like higher energy bills, rooms that never reach the setpoint, or one repair after another, it's smart to start planning a replacement. A planned replacement costs less and is far less stressful than scrambling for a new system during the worst heat wave of the year.

If comfort problems do show up, acting quickly keeps them small. A weak capacitor is a quick fix. The same capacitor left alone for a season can take the compressor with it and turn a minor repair into a full system replacement. It's smart to keep our number saved on your phone so you can reach us at the first sign of trouble. Early intervention saves time, money, and miserable nights.
