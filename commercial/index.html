Safe Installation

Safe Installations, Documented Service

Safety is part of every commercial job we do. Our technicians inspect heat exchangers for cracks, verify proper venting and combustion air, check electrical connections and disconnects, document the refrigerant charge, protect against condensate overflow, and check ventilation rates in occupied areas. The goal is straightforward: no carbon monoxide hazards, no poor air quality, no refrigerant leaks, and no ceiling tiles ruined by an overflowing drain. Findings go into a written report with priority levels and specific recommended actions.

This work serves a dual purpose: it protects people and property, and it provides the documentation that demonstrates due diligence to landlords, insurers, and auditors. Building owners benefit from maintaining regular professional HVAC service and acting on expert recommendations. We maintain detailed service logs, temperature and filter records, and a documentation binder for every account, with more frequent service for heavy-use equipment in kitchens, gyms, and large mechanical rooms.

Emergency Service

Rapid-Response HVAC Repair for Businesses

An HVAC emergency on a commercial property can shut down a kitchen, empty a retail floor on a summer afternoon, overheat a server room, and expose the owner to tenant disputes. Roanoke HVAC provides fast, emergency HVAC service for commercial properties across Roanoke and surrounding areas. When you call our commercial line, we dispatch an experienced technician quickly, because every hour without climate control costs you money.

Ongoing Protection

Scheduled HVAC Maintenance for Commercial Properties

Preventing failures is the most cost-effective way to manage commercial HVAC equipment. Every Roanoke HVAC program is customized to the property's equipment, usage, and budget, and covers scheduled inspections, coil cleaning, condensate line clearing, filter changes using the right MERV rating for the space, belt and bearing checks, refrigerant and static pressure testing, burner and heat exchanger inspection, and seasonal preparation before peak demand.

Fewer breakdowns, longer-lasting equipment, and lower energy costs: those are the results our commercial clients see after starting a program. The program typically costs a fraction of a single emergency replacement. Contact Roanoke HVAC to build a plan that fits your property and budget.
